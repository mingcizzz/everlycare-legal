Common questions

Password reset: tap "Forgot password" on the login screen — use the newest email we send (older reset links expire when a new one is requested).

Delete your account: in the app, Settings → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate. Details in our Privacy Policy.

常见问题

重置密码：在登录页点"忘记密码"——请使用最新收到的那封邮件（重新发起后，旧邮件里的链接会失效）。
