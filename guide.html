EverlyCare 快速上手指南

5 分钟学会核心用法。记住一句话：先记三天，规律自己会浮出来。

1. 注册与登录

打开 App → 注册 → 填邮箱、密码和称呼 → 勾选并阅读隐私政策 → 完成。
忘记密码：登录页点「忘记密码」→ 查邮箱 → 点最新那封邮件里的链接 → 在 App 里直接设置新密码。

2. 添加被照护者

首次进入会引导你创建被照护者档案：姓名、出生年份、与您的关系等。创建时会请您确认：您有权为这位家人记录照护信息（代理同意）。
💡 信息可以随时在「档案」里补充修改，先填最基本的就能开始用。

3. 日常记录（最核心的功能）

主页底部的大按钮是一切的入口：如厕、排便、餐食、喝水、用药、情绪……点开对应类型，几秒钟记完。
· 如厕/排便：记下时间就有价值；量、形态（Bristol 分型）能选就选；
· 喝水：主页有快捷水量按钮，点一下就记上（长按可自定义常用量）；
· 意外（失禁）：如实记录，不用自责——它正是发现规律最重要的线索。
💡 记录贵在坚持而不在完美。每天记 4 次以上，坚持一周，「分析」页就开始有你家老人自己的规律了。

4. 提醒 + 通知上直接记录

「提醒」页可为喝水、用药等设置提醒。提醒通知弹出时：
· 直接按通知上的「记录」按钮——不用解锁进 App 就记好了；
· 或点通知本体 → 打开已经预填好的记录面板确认。

5. 睡眠时段

在被照护者档案里设置 TA 的日常睡眠时段。之后夜里主页会切换成安静的夜景，预测和提醒都会避开休息时间——不会半夜拿提醒吵醒人。

6. 看懂如厕预测

主页的预测卡片显示下一次可能的如厕时间窗（会参考近期记录、餐食、睡眠，以及本地天气——天气需要定位权限，拒绝也完全不影响使用）。
颜色代表紧迫度：绿色从容 → 黄色临近 → 红色超时。在预期时间前主动引导如厕，是减少意外最有效的办法。
⚠️ 预测是照护辅助，不是医疗建议。它帮您安排日常，不能替代医生判断。

7. 桌面小组件

长按手机桌面空白处 → 左上角 ➕ → 搜索 EverlyCare → 添加小号或中号组件。
· 一眼看到：今日记录数、下一个预测时段、正在照护谁（名字在左上角）；
· 中号组件可以直接记录：点按钮原地记上，出现「✓ 已记录」就成功了；
· 「坐够计时」帮老人在马桶上坐满时间：点计时 → 倒计时 → 完成即记录。

8. 邀请家人共同照护

让家人也能记录和查看：
① 家人先注册自己的账号 → 在「设置」里复制自己的 User ID → 微信发给你；
② 你在「照护团队」页点右下角 ➕ → 粘贴 TA 的 ID → 发出邀请；
③ 家人在「照护团队」页接受邀请。之后大家的记录汇在一起，谁记的都看得到。

9. 历史与报告

「历史」页有月历：记过的日子有标记，点任何一天回看当天记录（长按记录可以修改）。「分析」页看趋势。需要给医生看时，可以导出 PDF 报告。

10. 隐私与账号

· 隐私政策：设置 → 隐私政策（中英双语）；
· 删除账号：设置 → 删除账号，立即生效；
· 有任何问题：设置 → 联系我们，或写信到 everlycarecore@googlegroups.com——每封都会看。

EverlyCare Quick Start Guide

Learn the essentials in 5 minutes. One thing to remember: log three days — patterns show up faster than you'd think.

1. Sign up & sign in

Open the app → Register → email, password, display name → read & accept the privacy policy.
Forgot your password? Tap "Forgot password" on the login screen → check your email → tap the link in the newest email → set a new password right in the app.

2. Add the person you care for

You'll be guided to create a care-recipient profile: name, birth year, your relationship. You'll confirm that you're entitled to record care information for them (proxy consent).

3. Daily logging (the heart of the app)

The big button at the bottom of Home is your entry point: toileting, bowel, meals, fluids, medication, mood — each takes seconds.
· Toileting/bowel: the time alone is valuable; add amount and Bristol type when you can;
· Fluids: quick water presets on Home — one tap logs it (long-press to customize);
· Accidents: log them honestly, no guilt — they're the most important clue to the pattern.
💡 Consistency beats perfection. Log 4+ times a day for a week and Insights starts showing your person's own rhythm.

4. Reminders + logging from the notification

Set water/medication reminders in the Reminders tab. When one fires:
· tap the Log button on the notification — done without unlocking;
· or tap the notification body to open a pre-filled log sheet.

5. Sleep window

Set their usual sleep hours in the profile. Home shifts to a quiet night scene, and predictions and reminders respect their rest.

6. Reading the toileting prediction

The Home card shows the next likely toileting window (based on recent logs, meals, sleep — and local weather if you grant optional location; declining changes nothing else).
Colors = urgency: green calm → yellow approaching → red overdue. Guiding them to the bathroom before the window is the single best way to reduce accidents.
⚠️ Predictions are caregiving aids, not medical advice.

7. Home-screen widget

Long-press your home screen → ➕ → search EverlyCare → add Small or Medium.
· At a glance: today's counts, next predicted window, whose care you're viewing (name top-left);
· The Medium widget logs directly — tap a button, see "✓ Logged";
· Seated timer: start → live countdown → Done logs it.

8. Invite family to the care team

① They register their own account → Settings → copy their User ID → send it to you;
② You: Care Team tab → ➕ → paste their ID → invite;
③ They accept in their Care Team tab. Everyone's logs flow into one shared record.

9. History & reports

History has a month calendar — logged days are marked; tap any day to review (long-press a log to edit). Insights shows trends. Export a PDF report for doctor visits.

10. Privacy & account

· Privacy policy: Settings → Privacy Policy (EN/中文);
· Delete account: Settings → Delete Account, effective immediately;
· Questions: Settings → Contact Us, or everlycarecore@googlegroups.com — we read every email.
